6B — Evidence-based finding disposition and adjudication

1. Preserve each original finding, affected requirements, uncertainty, proposed disposition,
   supporting evidence and authorized producer/run/candidate provenance. Reuse existing
   structured findings, challenge and disposition contracts where they fit.
2. Require reproduction or a supported source trace before upholding, refuting or otherwise
   disposing a finding. Allow evidence-based rejection of an incorrect reviewer assertion;
   do not blindly patch every assertion. Uncertainty remains unresolved when evidence is
   insufficient. Use the supported challenge/adjudication path for disagreement.

   under the applicable policy and authority; severity alone is not permission to dismiss it.

   routing. Routine engineering judgment should progress without repeated user approval.
   Genuine business decisions, exceptional authority and investigated external unknowns
   retain their separate trusted path.

   Cancellation, budget limits and repeated attempts cannot manufacture acceptance.
